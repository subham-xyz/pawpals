How matching works

A volunteer is suggested for your request only when all of these are true:

  • They live in the same area as your pet.
  • They offer the type of help you asked for.
  • They have been verified by an admin.
  • They have not been blocked.

Pick a volunteer from the dropdown and press Accept. After the job, press Mark completed and leave a rating.
